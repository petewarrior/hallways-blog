Spatial computing moves digital experiences out of screens and into the world around us.

## What is Spatial Computing?

At its core, spatial computing recognizes that humans think and navigate in three dimensions. By placing digital content in physical space, we create more intuitive interactions.

## Key Technologies

### Mixed Reality Headsets
Devices like Meta Quest, Apple Vision Pro, and Microsoft HoloLens enable immersive experiences that blend digital and physical worlds.

### Hand and Eye Tracking
Advanced sensors now allow computers to understand where we're looking and what our hands are doing, enabling natural gestures and interactions.

### Spatial Audio
Sound that responds to head movement and position creates more immersive environments.

## Industry Applications

- **Architecture**: Walk through buildings before they're built
- **Medicine**: Visualize patient data in 3D space
- **Manufacturing**: Train employees on complex machinery
- **Education**: Create immersive learning environments

## The Future

As hardware improves and costs decrease, spatial computing will become increasingly mainstream, much like smartphones did in the 2010s.
